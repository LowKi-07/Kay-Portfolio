Reimagining
Stripe Atlas for
global founders.

A complete overhaul of the Stripe Atlas dashboard, transforming a fragmented onboarding flow into a unified experience for entrepreneurs incorporating across 142 countries.

  • The Problem

— Chapter 01

Context: A product that scaled faster than it could simplify.

Stripe Atlas helps founders incorporate companies remotely. After six years of growth, the dashboard had become a patchwork of features — powerful, but overwhelming for first-time founders navigating tax filings, equity, and compliance.

— Chapter 02

The problem in numbers.

Internal data revealed that 47% of founders dropped off during the activation flow. Support tickets were dominated by confusion over next steps.

— Chapter 03 04 slides

The process: from chaos to clarity.

We ran 23 user interviews, mapped 4 jobs-to-be-done, prototyped 11 flows, and ran 3 rounds of usability testing before committing to a direction.

01 / 04
— Chapter 04

The solution: a dashboard that guides, not overwhelms.

A new status-led architecture, a unified component system, and a "next best action" engine that surfaces what matters now.

— Chapter 05

Impact: shipped, measured, celebrated.

64%
Reduction in median onboarding time across 142 countries.
3.2×
Increase in completed activations within first 30 days.
+72
NPS lift on the post-activation survey, up from +18.
Kishore's redesign is the rare combination of strategic clarity and obsessive craft. She didn't just reshape Atlas — she taught our team how to think about onboarding.
